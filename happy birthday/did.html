chapter one — a letter for you

Read it slowly. I mean every word.

Some people have to go out into the world and find their best friend. I was born and mine was already there — older, wiser, and already holding the last piece of cake.

Thank you for the borrowed clothes (returned, mostly), the advice at 1 a.m., the cover stories only you could tell with a straight face, and for taking the blame for roughly forty percent of what I actually did.

You were my first teacher, my first hero, and you're still the first phone call I want to make when anything happens — good, bad, or so funny it simply cannot be texted.

Growing up with you was the luckiest thing that ever happened to me. Whatever I become, a large part of it will have your fingerprints all over it.

Happy birthday, Didi. I love you the most.

— your little one
